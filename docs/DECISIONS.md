# Design Decisions

Decisions on questions not (or not clearly) answered in `DESIGN.md`. Newest at the bottom.

## 2026-10-28 – Pre-M0 clarifications

### Confirmed by the owner
- **DESIGN.md location:** moved to `docs/DESIGN.md` (as referenced by CLAUDE.md).
- **Left behind / death during a shift:** the player is out for the rest of that shift and rejoins at the start of the next shift. Carried loot is lost, no other penalty. Solo: the shift ends, unbanked loot is lost, the run continues with the next shift. (DESIGN.md §2 updated; it previously said "rest of the cycle".)
- **Solo without Steam:** solo runs with no network transport at all (`OfflineMultiplayerPeer`, host = peer 1), so it works when Steam is not running. Steam is only required for hosting/joining online lobbies.
- **"Plärren" key:** a shout input that has the same in-game effect as a loud voice (scares Bergmandl, attracts Tatzelwurm). Makes the mechanic available before voice chat (M3) and for players without a microphone.
- **Berghittn reference:** the earlier project lives in `X:\Code\Projects\GeilesGame`. It may be read for reference; nothing is copied from it without telling the owner first.

### Chosen by Claude (change any time)
- **Base shift length:** 13 min, plus the solo/duo bonus from §8. Value lives in `balance.tres`.
- **Base quota:** set during M1 playtests, lives in `balance.tres`.
- **Medium items:** carried in the hands (like heavy items, but at normal speed). The 4 inventory slots hold light items and tools only.
- **Build version:** `application/config/version` in `project.godot`; it is also the `ver` lobby metadata value.
- **Max players:** a single constant (default 4) so 6 stays possible.
- **Salzweibl voice lure:** a setting lets players opt out of having their voice recorded for it (the creature falls back to generic lines).

## 2026-10-28 – Creature voices

### Confirmed by the owner
- **Creatures talk**, as in Berghittn ("Gredn"): short dialect lines, **audio only, no text on screen** (no labels, no subtitles). Added to DESIGN.md §6 "Creature voices" and §10.
- **Sample check:** each creature's voice is approved from a sample pack (several filter variants) before it is final.

### Chosen by Claude (change any time)
- **Voice pipeline:** eSpeak NG + one filter per creature, generated by a Python script in `tools/audio/`, adapted from Berghittn's `tools/audio/voices.py` / `dsp.py` (owner told 2026-10-28, per the rule above). eSpeak is a dev tool only; only the `.ogg` files ship.
- **Who talks:** Bergmandl, Kasermandl (only while in darkness), Schichtmeister, Percht (a few words). Tatzelwurm does not speak. Salzweibl keeps its own voice-lure design.
- **Setting "Gegner reden"** turns speech off but keeps non-speech creature sounds, since those are gameplay cues.

## 2026-10-28 – M0 setup (chosen by Claude)
- **Engine:** Godot 4.7.2 stable, Forward+.
- **Quota payment:** sold loot becomes money; at the end of a cycle the quota is paid out of the money (money −= quota). Not enough money → fired. So spending in the shop mid-cycle is a risk. Score = cycles survived + total money earned.
- **Per-player balance tables** are `Array[float]` (double precision), not `PackedFloat32Array`: float32 turned 150 × 0.45 into 67.49…
- **GodotSteam** is not downloaded until M3. Until then `SteamBackend` reports itself unavailable and the default `--net=steam` falls back to ENet (logged as `[net] Steam backend not available, using ENet`).
- **Dev command-line flags** (after `--`): `--solo`, `--port=<n>` and `--smoke=<seconds>` (quit after that long, for headless smoke tests), in addition to `--net`, `--host`, `--join`.
- **Known engine noise:** if several ENet clients disconnect in the very same frame, the host logs `Unable to send packet on channel 0` (it relays "peer left" to a peer that is itself disconnecting). Harmless; `tools/mp_test.ps1` staggers client quits by 1 s.
