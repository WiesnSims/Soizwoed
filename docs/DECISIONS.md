# Design Decisions

Decisions on questions not (or not clearly) answered in `DESIGN.md`. Newest at the bottom.

## 2026-10-28 – Pre-M0 clarifications

### Confirmed by the owner
- **DESIGN.md location:** moved to `docs/DESIGN.md` (as referenced by CLAUDE.md).
- **Left behind / death during a shift:** the player is out for the rest of that shift and rejoins at the start of the next shift. Carried loot is lost, no other penalty. Solo: the shift ends, unbanked loot is lost, the run continues with the next shift. (DESIGN.md §2 updated; it previously said "rest of the cycle".)
- **Solo without Steam:** solo runs with no network transport at all (`OfflineMultiplayerPeer`, host = peer 1), so it works when Steam is not running. Steam is only required for hosting/joining online lobbies.
- **"Plärren" key:** a shout input that has the same in-game effect as a loud voice (scares Bergmandl, attracts Tatzelwurm). Makes the mechanic available before voice chat (M3) and for players without a microphone.
- **Berghittn reference:** the earlier project lives in `X:\Code\Projects\GeilesGame`. It may be read for reference; nothing is copied from it without telling the owner first.

### Chosen by Claude (change any time)
- **Base shift length:** 13 min, plus the solo/duo bonus from §8. Value lives in `balance.tres`.
- **Base quota:** set during M1 playtests, lives in `balance.tres`.
- **Medium items:** carried in the hands (like heavy items, but at normal speed). The 4 inventory slots hold light items and tools only.
- **Build version:** `application/config/version` in `project.godot`; it is also the `ver` lobby metadata value.
- **Max players:** a single constant (default 4) so 6 stays possible.
- **Salzweibl voice lure:** a setting lets players opt out of having their voice recorded for it (the creature falls back to generic lines).

## 2026-10-28 – M0 setup (chosen by Claude)
- **Engine:** Godot 4.7.2 stable, Forward+.
- **Quota payment:** sold loot becomes money; at the end of a cycle the quota is paid out of the money (money −= quota). Not enough money → fired. So spending in the shop mid-cycle is a risk. Score = cycles survived + total money earned.
- **Per-player balance tables** are `Array[float]` (double precision), not `PackedFloat32Array`: float32 turned 150 × 0.45 into 67.49…
- **GodotSteam** is not downloaded until M3. Until then `SteamBackend` reports itself unavailable and the default `--net=steam` falls back to ENet (logged as `[net] Steam backend not available, using ENet`).
- **Dev command-line flags** (after `--`): `--solo`, `--port=<n>` and `--smoke=<seconds>` (quit after that long, for headless smoke tests), in addition to `--net`, `--host`, `--join`.
- **Known engine noise:** if several ENet clients disconnect in the very same frame, the host logs `Unable to send packet on channel 0` (it relays "peer left" to a peer that is itself disconnecting). Harmless; `tools/mp_test.ps1` staggers client quits by 1 s.

## 2026-10-29 – M1 solo vertical slice (chosen by Claude, change any time)

### Structure
- **One game scene** (`game/game.tscn`, the main scene): the Gasthaus stands 400 m above the mine, and the main menu is an overlay on it, with the Gasthaus behind it as the background. All network node paths exist from the first frame, so clients never get packets for nodes they have not loaded yet. Leaving a session reloads the scene.
- **The lift ride** is a fade to black, the ride sounds, and a move of everything in the cage to the other cage. The mine is built on every peer during the fade (about 0.5–0.8 s).
- **Spawning:** players, items and creatures come from the host through `MultiplayerSpawner`s. Positions of players and creatures are synced; items, carrying together and spectating are M2.
- **Late join:** a client that connects during a shift is sent back to the menu with a message (DESIGN.md §9: late join only in the hub).

### Shift rules
- **Ending a shift early:** hold the lever inside the mine's Förderkorb for 1.5 s. You must stand in the cage, so nobody pulls it by accident. The lift going up ends the shift for everyone; whoever is not in the cage is left behind (§2).
- **What rides up and gets sold:** loot lying in the cage, loot held by a player in the cage, and loot in a Schubkarre that is in the cage or pushed by a rider. Everything else in the mine is lost, including tools left down there. Tools that ride up stay yours.
- **Lamp:** the carbide only burns in the mine. The Karbid barrel in both cages refills it for free; the Karbidpatrone from the shop refills 60 % on the way.
- **Death:** nothing in M1 can kill you (the Bergmandl is not deadly). Health, death and spectating arrive with the deadly creatures in M2/M4.

### Balance (first guess, tune in playtests)
- **Money** is shown in Schilling ("S"), for the Austrian flavour.
- **Quota base 1500:** cycle 1 solo = 675 S over 3 shifts. A solo shift has about 10 loot items worth about 60 S each, so you need roughly 40 % of everything the mine holds.
- **Shop prices:** Spitzhacke 150 S, Karbidpatrone 40 S, Schubkarre 250 S. Every purchase risks the quota.

### Bergmandl
- Spots players and greets them. Goes for loot lying around or in a parked Schubkarre. Tries to snatch loot from hands or the top of a pushed Schubkarre (35 % per attempt, every 2 s). Then runs to a far waypoint and drops it there.
- **Stash:** loot a Bergmandl dropped after running off is left alone by all Bergmandl until a player picks it up again. Without this they kept stealing it back from each other.
- **Loot inside the Förderkorb is safe.** That makes the cage the place to collect loot.
- 30 HP: the Spitzhacke hits for 34 (one hit), a bare-hand "Watschn" for 8. Hit or shouted at (Plärren, 14 m), it drops what it carries and runs away for 8 s.
- Groups of 2–3 spawn at least 2 chunks deep and 15 m from players, half the budget at the start and the rest every 90 s. A player-count factor scales the budget (§8).
- Its voice and footsteps are muffled when rock is between it and you (a simple raycast low-pass). Proximity voice will reuse this later.

### Presentation
- **Gasthaus music:** a short accordion waltz ("Stubenmusi") rendered by `tools/audio/music.py`, playing from a corner of the room. No music in the mine (§10).
- **Dev flags** (after `--`): `--auto-shift` (ride down, play, ride up), `--scenario=mandl|loot` (scripted checks in `tools/dev/dev_scenario.gd`), `--shots=<dir>` (screenshots every 1.5 s, needs a window). The smoke test runs the shift and both scenarios headless.

## 2026-10-29 – M2 multiplayer via ENet (chosen by Claude, change any time)

### Sync
- **Players** send their position, body yaw and head pitch about 20 times a second; everyone else shows them 100 ms in the past, blended between snapshots (`net/net_interpolator.gd`). Lamp on/off, carbide left, crouching and the selected pocket slot are synced too, so you see another miner's lamp, crouch and the tool in their hand. Creatures use the same smoothing (host → clients).
- **Free items** are simulated on the host only. Clients keep them still and follow the host's transforms (moving items ~20×/s, a final reliable update when they come to rest). Held, carted and stolen items follow their hand / slot on every peer as before. This is how "held items are owned by the holder" from DESIGN.md §11 works out: the holder's own hand moves them, with no network delay.
- **Joining in the Gasthaus:** the host sends the joiner where every item is and who holds it.
- **Teleports** (the lift ride): position packets from the old place still on the way are ignored for half a second, so nobody gets yanked back.

### Carrying together
- Look at a heavy item another miner holds and press E: you take the other end. Both of you then walk at 95 % speed (alone: 50 %). No sprinting with it.
- More than 2.8 m apart and the helper loses their grip ("Auskemma!"). G lets go of your end; when the main carrier lets go, the helper carries on alone.
- A Bergmandl does not try to snatch what two miners carry.

### Health, death and spectating
- **Health belongs to the host.** In M2 the only way to die is a hard fall (landing faster than 8 m/s, about a 3.3 m drop; 12 health per m/s above that). The deadly creatures in M4 use the same path (`Game.host_damage_player`). Nobody gets hurt in the Gasthaus.
- **Death:** everything you carry drops, your body becomes a heavy loot item, "Hiniger Kumpl (Sterbegeld)", worth 60 S when it rides up (it can be carried together or wheeled in the Schubkarre). You sink to the floor, and after 2.5 s you watch a living teammate through their eyes; click or the mouse wheel switches between them.
- **Everybody dead:** 4 s later the lift goes up and the shift ends (solo: 4 s after your death).
- **Names:** players are "Kumpl 1–4" in the order they joined (Steam names come with M3). A name shows over a teammate's head only while you look right at them from up to 10 m, so it never lights up the dark.

### Player count
- **Scaling (§8) uses the players present when the lift goes down.** In the Gasthaus it follows joins and leaves, so the quota on the notice board changes when someone joins. A cycle's quota can therefore change between shifts if the crew changes.
- **The free Schubkarre** is a solo extra: a host who opens a multiplayer game does not get it, even before anyone joins.

### Testing
- `tools/mp_test.ps1 -Clients 3` now plays a short shift: the host waits for all clients (`--wait-peers=<n>`), rides down, rides up after a few seconds (`--auto-shift=<s>`), and every peer must have built the same mine.
- `tools/mp_test.ps1 -Coop` runs the scripted co-op scenario (`--scenario=coop` on the host, `coopclient` on the client): a falling item seen on the client, carrying together, hand-over, death and spectating.

## 2026-10-29 – M3 Steam (chosen by Claude, change any time)

### GodotSteam
- **GodotSteam GDExtension 4.23.2** (Steamworks 1.65, from Codeberg, built for Godot 4.4+, works with 4.7.2) as a plain addon: the stock Godot editor and export templates keep working. Only the Windows x64 libraries are in the repo; the addon's editor updater plugin is left out (it would check the web every time the editor opens).
- **Transport:** `SteamMultiplayerPeer` with `server_relay`: clients connect only to the host (lobby owner), the host relays – the same star layout as ENet. Steam's relay network handles NAT.

### Lobbies
- **Hosting** creates a **friends-only** lobby for 4. "Für olle sichtbor" next to the host button (or `--public`) makes it public. Every lobby carries `game=soizwoed`, `ver=<version>` and `name=<host's Steam name>`.
- **Joining:** "Partien suachn" lists public Soizwöd lobbies of the same version (search filtered on `game` and `ver`) plus the lobbies Steam friends are in (also checked for `game` and `ver`), marked "(Freind)". Joining a lobby of another game or version is refused with a message.
- **Invites:** in the game, Esc → "Freind eiladn" opens the Steam overlay invite dialog (Shift+Tab → friends list works too). Accepting an invite or "Spiel beitreten" on a friend: if the game is running, it leaves the current session (also solo) and joins; if not, Steam starts it with `+connect_lobby <id>` and it joins right away.
- **Late join:** the host makes the lobby unjoinable while the crew is down the mine, and joinable again in the Gasthaus (the old refusal stays as a safety net).
- **Version:** `application/config/version` is now **0.3.0** (M3). Builds of different versions never see each other's lobbies.
- **Without Steam** (not running, or `--net=enet`) the menu shows the old address field and everything runs over ENet. Headless runs default to ENet, so automated tests never show the Steam account as "playing Spacewar"; `--net=steam` forces Steam.

### Names
- With Steam, players show their **Steam name** (nameplate, spectator line, "… is hin!"). Without Steam they stay "Kumpl 1–4".

### Proximity voice
- **Push-to-talk on V** by default; Esc → "Redn: Mikro offn" switches to open mic (Steam's voice detection only sends while you speak). The choice is saved in `user://settings.cfg`.
- Heard from the speaker's head: full volume close by, fading out to 30 m (`voice_chat_range`), **muffled through rock** (same check as creature voices), a little cave reverb on the Voice bus.
- **Dead players** are heard only by other dead players (without position, they are spectating); the dead still hear the living.
- **Shouting into the mic counts as "Plärren":** louder than `voice_shout_level` (0.6 peak) for 0.25 s scares Bergmandl, with the shout key's cooldown. (The Tatzelwurm in M4 will use the same loudness.)
- **"Mikro probiern"** in the Esc menu plays your own voice back to you, to check the mic alone.
- "Du redst…" bottom right while your voice goes out.
- **ENet sessions have no voice** (DESIGN.md §11 allows that).

### Testing
- `tools/steam_test.ps1` (Steam running, one account): a headless host creates a public lobby, a second instance must find exactly it with the filtered search, and joining it must be refused (own account). Real joining and voice need a second account on another PC.

## 2026-10-29 – M4 start (chosen by Claude unless marked, change any time)

### Steam invites without the overlay
- **"Freind eiladn" did nothing** (owner report): it only opened the Steam overlay's invite dialog, and Steam hooks its overlay into a Vulkan game only when Steam itself starts the game. Soizwoed.exe started from its folder (the normal case with App ID 480) has no overlay, so Shift+Tab does nothing either.
- **Now:** if Steam reports the overlay as enabled, the button opens it as before; otherwise an in-game list of your Steam friends (online first, "(spüt grad)" for friends in Spacewar/Soizwöd) with an "Eiladn" button each, which sends a normal Steam lobby invite (`inviteUserToLobby`). The friend gets it in their Steam chat; accepting it starts or joins the game as before. DESIGN.md §9 already allowed "an in-game friends list".

### Settings menu (owner request, moved from M5 to M4)
- **"Einstellungen"** in the main menu and the Esc menu, four tabs, every change applied and saved right away to `user://settings.cfg` ("Standard" resets everything):
  - **Ton:** overall volume, music, sounds (effects, creatures and ambience together), teammates' voices, "Gegner redn" (DESIGN.md §6), open mic.
  - **Anzeige:** window / borderless fullscreen / exclusive fullscreen, VSync, frame rate cap (30–240 or none), field of view (60–100°, default 78), brightness (multiplies the tuned exposure; the mine stays meant to be dark).
  - **Grafik:** quality preset Niedrig / Mittel / Hoch / Ultra (shadow atlas and soft-shadow quality, SSAO, SSIL, volumetric fog resolution; "Hoch" is the look the game was tuned with), 3D resolution 50–100 % (FSR below 100 %), dust in the light (volumetric fog) on/off, anti-aliasing (off, FXAA, TAA, MSAA + TAA = the old default).
  - **Spü:** mouse sensitivity, invert mouse Y, head bob.
- Default window mode stays "Im Fenster", so local multi-instance tests don't open fullscreen. Headless and `--shots` runs never touch the window.
- Key rebinding and a language choice are left for M5 (only German exists so far).

### Taskbar icon (owner report)
- **Taskbar showed the generic Windows icon** while the file, the shortcut and the window corner showed the lamp: Godot 4.7.2's exporter rewrites the exe's icons but writes the icon group's name (`GODOT_ICON`) with a broken offset. Explorer just takes the first group, but the game loads its window-class icon by that name at startup, gets nothing and falls back to the generic icon, which the Windows 11 taskbar shows.
- **Now:** `tools/build.ps1` runs `tools/fix_exe_icon.ps1` after the export. It rewrites the exe's resources from `icon.ico` (all 7 sizes, Godot kept only 6), restores the group name and keeps version info and manifest. This only works because the pck is not embedded. An export straight from the editor still has the bug; use the build script.

### Creature spawning
- **Each biome lists its creatures** (`ChunkSet.creatures`). The host spends the shift's enemy budget on them by weight: half at the start, the rest every 90 s, as before. The rules (`CreatureSpawnRule`, in `balance.tres`) give each type a budget cost, a weight, a group size, the first cycle it appears and a maximum alive at once:
  - Bergmandl: cost 1, weight 3, groups of 2–3.
  - Tatzelwurm: cost 3, weight 1, at most 1.
  - Kasermandl: cost 2.5, weight 1, at most 1.
  - Schichtmeister: outside the budget, only after the whistle.
- **Mine sections** (`BiomeRule`): the Salzsee unlocks from cycle 2 (`unlock_cycle = 1`) with 1.35× loot values and 1.2× enemy budget.

### Noise (for the Tatzelwurm)
- Every noise has a reach in metres; creatures within it hear where it came from (`Game.host_noise`):
  - movement: sprinting 22, walking 6, crouching 0, pushing the Schubkarre 12;
  - heavy drop 20, shout or loud voice 25;
  - rail cart 20, Böller 35, bell 40, explosion 60, Alphorn 80.
- Movement is checked every 0.5 s.

### Tatzelwurm
- Underground it is invisible and only heard: grinding rock, muffled through walls. It roams slowly, and heads for the last noise it heard.
- It bursts out of the floor there and stays up about 6 s. It lunges (55 damage) at anyone within 3.2 m who moves faster than 2.2 m/s, so crouching or standing still is safe; walking is not. Then it dives and rests about 10 s.
- 120 HP. Any hit makes it dive at once, so the Spitzhacke chases it off but rarely kills it.

### Kasermandl
- **Lit = frozen:** it counts as lit when a lamp's light reaches it (within 90 % of the lamp's range, no rock in between – whichever way the player looks) or a hanging lantern is within 6 m. Lit, it stands still, falls silent and fades out; in the dark it fades back in.
- In the dark it creeps (2.2 m/s) after the nearest miner and rushes (4.8 m/s) at one whose lamp is off. A touch does 35 damage, then it slips back into the dark for 12 s.
- It cannot be hurt: the pick goes right through it. Light is the only counter. Close by in the dark you hear it breathe.

### Schichtmeister
- Comes 12 s after the whistle, once per shift, for the miner outside the cage who is nearest the lift. He appears 15–40 m behind them, on the side away from the cage.
- 6.4 m/s (faster than a sprint); one touch kills. He never enters the Förderkorb and vanishes when the lift goes up. Hits only annoy him. The Alphorn stuns him, and he won't walk into a "Bierzelt" cloud.

### Creature voices
- **Kasermandl and Schichtmeister use variant A** (picked by the owner after the M4 playtest).
- Each creature's `.tres` is generated from `tools/audio/voices.py` (`tools/audio/creature_tres.py`), so lines and files never drift apart.

### Creature movement
- A creature blocked by something (the parked mine cart, a crate) sidesteps for 0.7 s.
- If it makes no progress for 4 s, it hops back to the nearest waypoint. Without this, a Bergmandl could stay wedged at one spot for good.

### Tools (§7)
- **Using a tool:** select it in a pocket slot and click (left mouse). The HUD shows what the click does. Prices:
  - Stirnlampe 220 S, Alphorn 300 S, Funkgerät 120 S, Seil 90 S, Pfeiferl 80 S (tools: they stay yours for the run as long as they ride up with you).
  - Böller 30 S, Rauchbombe 60 S, Obstler 45 S, Leberkässemmel 55 S (used up).
  - Spitzhacke, Karbidpatrone and Schubkarre are unchanged.
- **Elektrische Stirnlampe:** click switches between carbide and the Stirnlampe. While it is on: no flame (safe in firedamp), steady colder light, a little more reach, no hiss. Its battery lasts 12 min and is charged in the Gasthaus before every shift. Lose it and you're back on carbide. The Kasermandl freezes in either light.
- **Alphorn** (a "travel size" one that fits a pocket): every creature within 20 m in a 70° cone in front, not behind rock, is stunned for 6 s and then reacts as if shouted at. The blast is a noise that carries 80 m, so a Tatzelwurm comes. 12 s until you have breath again.
- **Böller:** lit and thrown. After 2.5 s it bangs:
  - noise 35 m (draws the Tatzelwurm);
  - Bergmandl within 12 m run;
  - up to 20 damage within 3 m, to creatures and players, shielded by rock;
  - it is a flame: firedamp goes up.
- **Rauchbombe "Bierzelt":** thrown; 1.2 s later a 5 m cloud for 35 s. Creatures leave it and do not path into it. It does not hurt players.
- **Funkgerät:** if both you and a teammate carry one and you are more than 12 m apart (40 % of the voice range), you also hear each other out of the radio: tinny, never quieter than −6 dB, with static while a creature is within 10 m of either end. Click = a crackle on every other Funkgerät plus "… funkt di an!", which works without voice chat (ENet) too.
- **Obstler:** +25 health at once, the view sways and rolls for 20 s. **Leberkässemmel:** 4 s of eating (bar under the crosshair, cancelled if you switch slot or pick up something heavy), then +40.
- **Seil & Kletterhaken:** click while looking at the floor near an edge with a drop of more than 1.8 m: the rope hangs down from there (up to 7 m) for the rest of the shift. Climb it like a ladder: forward = up (down while looking down), back = down, jump = let go. With something heavy in your hands you climb at 40 % speed; never with a Schubkarre. Hold E on the hook to take the rope back.
- **Rail track and Hunt (Oberer Stollen):**
  - A track runs from just outside the Förderkorb along the main path, as far as the chunks allow (pits and water stop it). The decorative rails and parked carts on that path make way for it.
  - One Hunt stands at the lift. Press E to push it: it stays 1.4 m ahead of you on the track at 85 % walking speed, both ways; G or walking more than 3.2 m away lets go.
  - It holds 6 units of loot (a heavy item counts 3). It is loud (noise 20 m), and Bergmandl steal from it too.
  - It never rides up: unload it into the cage.
  - The Grubenhund-Pfeiferl calls it: it rolls along the track to the point nearest you at 3 m/s and stops 1.6 m short.
- **Salt walls:**
  - The generator closes half of the loop passages (shortcuts) and a quarter of the dead-end entrances (niches) with a salt wall.
  - The Spitzhacke breaks one in 3 hits (100 HP); bare hands only get "Do brauchst a Spitzhackn". A broken wall leaves 1–2 small salt crystals.
  - Creatures do not path through an unbroken wall, except the Tatzelwurm underground.

### Hazards (§4)
- **Placement:** every peer places firedamp and brine from the seed, in chunks two or more steps from the lift. The host decides what happens: ignition, rockfalls, the bridge giving way.
- **Schlagende Wetter (firedamp):**
  - A pocket is a 3 m sphere just under the roof, at the middle of a chunk. 30 % of chunks get one in cycle 1, plus 5 % per cycle (at most 80 %). Never in a chunk with a hanging lantern.
  - All you see is a faint shimmer, like hot air.
  - Within 2.5 m of a pocket the carbide flame flares up tall and bluish, with a whoosh: the old miners' warning sign.
  - A carbide flame inside the pocket for 1 s sets it off: up to 90 damage within 7 m (rock shields), the mine hears it 60 m away, the camera shakes. Bangs and other explosions also set pockets off, so pockets can chain.
  - Pass with the lamp off (in the dark, with the Kasermandl) or with the Stirnlampe.
- **Steinschlag (rockfall):**
  - About every 75 s (±30 %), with a 50 % chance, the roof gives at a waypoint 3–12 m from a random miner outside the station. You hear creaking and see grit trickling down; 2.5 s later the rock comes down: 45 damage within 2.2 m.
  - In 35 % of cases it leaves a pile blocking the tunnel (60 HP: 2 pick hits). It only blocks if:
    - every miner still has another way back to the lift;
    - the spot is not a crossing;
    - nobody stands where the pile would be.
- **Gesenk (new Oberer Stollen chunk, 2+ steps deep, no rails, no gas or brine):**
  - A 5 m deep pit in a small room, with loot at the bottom. Narrow ledges along the sides lead around it.
  - A rotten plank bridge crosses it. The bridge creaks under anyone and gives way under someone carrying something heavy: a salt block, a body, or a Schubkarre with a heavy load. A heavy item dropped on it breaks it too.
  - Two rotten ladders lead down. They creak, and give way after 0.6 s of climbing with something heavy in your hands.
  - The fall costs about 25 health. The Seil is the safe way up with a load.
- **Sole (brine):** pools of 2.2 m radius in 12 % of chunks (more in the Salzsee). Wading through one slows you to 60 %, the carbide burns 3× as fast while you are in it, and footsteps slosh.

### Salzsee (§4) and the Grubnplan
- **Picking a section:** a yellowed "Grubnplan" hangs right of the notice board in the Gasthaus. E opens the list of sections: the Oberer Stollen from the start, the Salzsee from quota cycle 2.
  - Anyone in the crew can pick; the host checks the pick is unlocked.
  - The choice holds for the following shifts until someone changes it, and the plan shows "Heit: …".
  - A new run starts in the Oberer Stollen again.
- **The Salzsee:**
  - **Layout:** 16 m cells; passages 5 m wide and 4.5 m high. Tunnels, a grotto with a salt column, a "Salzdom" (12 × 9 × 12 m with two columns), and the lake cavern. No rails.

## 2026-10-30 – M6 playtest feedback

### Asked for by the owner
- **Movement stutter and wriggling held items – fixed.** Cause: movement ran in 60 Hz physics steps while the screen draws at its own rate, so the camera jumped unevenly ("physics jitter"); held items were smoothed towards that jumping camera and wriggled. Now:
  - physics interpolation is on (with Godot's jitter fix off, as recommended for interpolation);
  - the camera is placed every rendered frame from the interpolated body, with the current mouse look (no input delay);
  - held items follow their point exactly;
  - things moved per frame (remote players, clients' creatures and items) opt out of interpolation.
  - Measured while walking (`--scenario=smooth`): frame-to-frame unevenness 117 % → 0 %.
- **No 60 fps cap by default any more:** VSync follows the screen. A 60 cap on a 144 Hz screen paces frames unevenly. Settings files that still have the old 60 default move to "no cap" once.
- **Aiming down the sights:** the gun comes up so its sight line lies exactly on the screen centre, where the shots go (bead 0.07° off in the check). The pistol and the shotgun have a rear notch and a front bead. The crosshair hides while aiming, and the gun glides between hip and eye. A red-dot sight can come later as an upgrade.
- **Slots:** keys 1–3 weapons (guns and the Spitzhacke), 4 a throwable (Böller, Rauchbombe; a second one goes into a pocket), 5–9 everything else. The owner's message said "4 for items" but "5–9 items"; we went with 5 item slots so every number key has a slot. The mouse wheel skips empty slots.
- **HUD like other shooters:**
  - a hotbar of 9 squares with icons rendered from each item's own 3D model, the key number, and the magazine count on guns, in three groups; the selected slot is lit and its name shows briefly when switching;
  - health (with its number), lamp and breath bars with drawn icons, bottom left;
  - a large magazine count, the spare rounds and a bullet or shell icon, bottom right.
- **More ammo** (the misaligned sights wasted a lot of it): ration 24 pistol rounds per shift (was 16), 3.5 boxes per miner in the mine (was 2.5), pistol boxes of 12 (was 10).

### Microphone and audio devices (owner request)
- **Why the mic seemed dead:**
  - voice only worked in Steam sessions;
  - it used Steam's own recorder, so the microphone was whatever the Steam client was set to (Steam → Settings → Voice);
  - it is push-to-talk on V by default;
  - solo and ENet games had no voice at all.
- **Now the game records the microphone itself** (`Mic` autoload): from the input device picked in the settings, 16 kHz mono, μ-law coded (~16 KB/s while talking), sent over the game's own connection. Voice works the same over Steam and ENet, so it can be tested locally.
  - Push-to-talk V or open mic (open mic now sends only while you are louder than a small gate).
  - The mic only records while voice chat runs or the sound settings are open, and never in automated runs.
- **Settings → Ton:** "Lautsprecher / Kopfhörer" (output device) and "Mikrofon" (input device), both "Standard vom System" by default, and a live mic level meter. A device that is unplugged falls back to the default.
