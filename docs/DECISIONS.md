# Design Decisions

Decisions on questions not (or not clearly) answered in `DESIGN.md`. Newest at the bottom.

## 2026-10-28 – Pre-M0 clarifications

### Confirmed by the owner
- **DESIGN.md location:** moved to `docs/DESIGN.md` (as referenced by CLAUDE.md).
- **Left behind / death during a shift:** the player is out for the rest of that shift and rejoins at the start of the next shift. Carried loot is lost, no other penalty. Solo: the shift ends, unbanked loot is lost, the run continues with the next shift. (DESIGN.md §2 updated; it previously said "rest of the cycle".)
- **Solo without Steam:** solo runs with no network transport at all (`OfflineMultiplayerPeer`, host = peer 1), so it works when Steam is not running. Steam is only required for hosting/joining online lobbies.
- **"Plärren" key:** a shout input that has the same in-game effect as a loud voice (scares Bergmandl, attracts Tatzelwurm). Makes the mechanic available before voice chat (M3) and for players without a microphone.
- **Berghittn reference:** the earlier project lives in `X:\Code\Projects\GeilesGame`. It may be read for reference; nothing is copied from it without telling the owner first.

### Chosen by Claude (change any time)
- **Base shift length:** 13 min, plus the solo/duo bonus from §8. Value lives in `balance.tres`.
- **Base quota:** set during M1 playtests, lives in `balance.tres`.
- **Medium items:** carried in the hands (like heavy items, but at normal speed). The 4 inventory slots hold light items and tools only.
- **Build version:** `application/config/version` in `project.godot`; it is also the `ver` lobby metadata value.
- **Max players:** a single constant (default 4) so 6 stays possible.
- **Salzweibl voice lure:** a setting lets players opt out of having their voice recorded for it (the creature falls back to generic lines).

## 2026-10-28 – Creature voices

### Confirmed by the owner
- **Creatures talk**, as in Berghittn ("Gredn"): short dialect lines, **audio only, no text on screen** (no labels, no subtitles). Added to DESIGN.md §6 "Creature voices" and §10.
- **Sample check:** each creature's voice is approved from a sample pack (several filter variants) before it is final.

### Chosen by Claude (change any time)
- **Voice pipeline:** eSpeak NG + one filter per creature, generated by a Python script in `tools/audio/`, adapted from Berghittn's `tools/audio/voices.py` / `dsp.py` (owner told 2026-10-28, per the rule above). eSpeak is a dev tool only; only the `.ogg` files ship.
- **Who talks:** Bergmandl, Kasermandl (only while in darkness), Schichtmeister, Percht (a few words). Tatzelwurm does not speak. Salzweibl keeps its own voice-lure design.
- **Setting "Gegner reden"** turns speech off but keeps non-speech creature sounds, since those are gameplay cues.

## 2026-10-28 – M0 setup (chosen by Claude)
- **Engine:** Godot 4.7.2 stable, Forward+.
- **Quota payment:** sold loot becomes money; at the end of a cycle the quota is paid out of the money (money −= quota). Not enough money → fired. So spending in the shop mid-cycle is a risk. Score = cycles survived + total money earned.
- **Per-player balance tables** are `Array[float]` (double precision), not `PackedFloat32Array`: float32 turned 150 × 0.45 into 67.49…
- **GodotSteam** is not downloaded until M3. Until then `SteamBackend` reports itself unavailable and the default `--net=steam` falls back to ENet (logged as `[net] Steam backend not available, using ENet`).
- **Dev command-line flags** (after `--`): `--solo`, `--port=<n>` and `--smoke=<seconds>` (quit after that long, for headless smoke tests), in addition to `--net`, `--host`, `--join`.
- **Known engine noise:** if several ENet clients disconnect in the very same frame, the host logs `Unable to send packet on channel 0` (it relays "peer left" to a peer that is itself disconnecting). Harmless; `tools/mp_test.ps1` staggers client quits by 1 s.

## 2026-10-29 – M1 solo vertical slice (chosen by Claude, change any time)

### Structure
- **One game scene** (`game/game.tscn`, the main scene): the Gasthaus stands 400 m above the mine, and the main menu is an overlay on it, with the Gasthaus behind it as the background. All network node paths exist from the first frame, so clients never get packets for nodes they have not loaded yet. Leaving a session reloads the scene.
- **The lift ride** is a fade to black, the ride sounds, and a move of everything in the cage to the other cage. The mine is built on every peer during the fade (about 0.5–0.8 s).
- **Spawning:** players, items and creatures come from the host through `MultiplayerSpawner`s. Positions of players and creatures are synced; items, carrying together and spectating are M2.
- **Late join:** a client that connects during a shift is sent back to the menu with a message (DESIGN.md §9: late join only in the hub).

### Shift rules
- **Ending a shift early:** hold the lever inside the mine's Förderkorb for 1.5 s. You must stand in the cage, so nobody pulls it by accident. The lift going up ends the shift for everyone; whoever is not in the cage is left behind (§2).
- **What rides up and gets sold:** loot lying in the cage, loot held by a player in the cage, and loot in a Schubkarre that is in the cage or pushed by a rider. Everything else in the mine is lost, including tools left down there. Tools that ride up stay yours.
- **Lamp:** the carbide only burns in the mine. The Karbid barrel in both cages refills it for free; the Karbidpatrone from the shop refills 60 % on the way.
- **Death:** nothing in M1 can kill you (the Bergmandl is not deadly). Health, death and spectating arrive with the deadly creatures in M2/M4.

### Balance (first guess, tune in playtests)
- **Money** is shown in Schilling ("S"), for the Austrian flavour.
- **Quota base 1500:** cycle 1 solo = 675 S over 3 shifts. A solo shift has about 10 loot items worth about 60 S each, so you need roughly 40 % of everything the mine holds.
- **Shop prices:** Spitzhacke 150 S, Karbidpatrone 40 S, Schubkarre 250 S. Every purchase risks the quota.

### Bergmandl
- Spots players and greets them. Goes for loot lying around or in a parked Schubkarre. Tries to snatch loot from hands or the top of a pushed Schubkarre (35 % per attempt, every 2 s). Then runs to a far waypoint and drops it there.
- **Stash:** loot a Bergmandl dropped after running off is left alone by all Bergmandl until a player picks it up again. Without this they kept stealing it back from each other.
- **Loot inside the Förderkorb is safe.** That makes the cage the place to collect loot.
- 30 HP: the Spitzhacke hits for 34 (one hit), a bare-hand "Watschn" for 8. Hit or shouted at (Plärren, 14 m), it drops what it carries and runs away for 8 s.
- Groups of 2–3 spawn at least 2 chunks deep and 15 m from players, half the budget at the start and the rest every 90 s. A player-count factor scales the budget (§8).
- Its voice and footsteps are muffled when rock is between it and you (a simple raycast low-pass). Proximity voice will reuse this later.

### Presentation
- **Gasthaus music:** a short accordion waltz ("Stubenmusi") rendered by `tools/audio/music.py`, playing from a corner of the room. No music in the mine (§10).
- **Dev flags** (after `--`): `--auto-shift` (ride down, play, ride up), `--scenario=mandl|loot` (scripted checks in `tools/dev/dev_scenario.gd`), `--shots=<dir>` (screenshots every 1.5 s, needs a window). The smoke test runs the shift and both scenarios headless.

## 2026-10-29 – M1 playtest feedback

### Confirmed by the owner
- **Bergmandl voice:** variant A ("Kobold") is final.

### Chosen by Claude (change any time)
- **Loading the Schubkarre:** besides E (loot in your hands, or loot on the floor while pushing), you can aim at the barrow and press G to put in whatever you hold, pocket items included. Loot that lands in the bed and lies still is loaded on its own, so dropping or throwing it in works too.
- **Held items** follow the hand every rendered frame with a little smoothing, instead of every physics step. That was what made them shake when turning.
- **Your own lamp is silent;** you only hear other players' lamps hiss.
- **Footsteps:** the stride grows with speed: about 2 steps/s walking, 2.5 sprinting, 1.3 crouching. Head bob follows the steps.
- **Light falloff:** lamp and lantern attenuation 2.0 (was 1.3/1.4), ambient light in the mine cut to a third, less bounce light. Darker away from light sources.
- **Bergmandl look:** Poly Haven fabric/leather textures (wool coat, fleece cap, fur beard, leather boots, belt and bag) plus moustache and brows.

## 2026-10-29 – M2 multiplayer via ENet (chosen by Claude, change any time)

### Sync
- **Players** send their position, body yaw and head pitch about 20 times a second; everyone else shows them 100 ms in the past, blended between snapshots (`net/net_interpolator.gd`). Lamp on/off, carbide left, crouching and the selected pocket slot are synced too, so you see another miner's lamp, crouch and the tool in their hand. Creatures use the same smoothing (host → clients).
- **Free items** are simulated on the host only. Clients keep them still and follow the host's transforms (moving items ~20×/s, a final reliable update when they come to rest). Held, carted and stolen items follow their hand / slot on every peer as before. This is how "held items are owned by the holder" from DESIGN.md §11 works out: the holder's own hand moves them, with no network delay.
- **Joining in the Gasthaus:** the host sends the joiner where every item is and who holds it.
- **Teleports** (the lift ride): position packets from the old place still on the way are ignored for half a second, so nobody gets yanked back.

### Carrying together
- Look at a heavy item another miner holds and press E: you take the other end. Both of you then walk at 95 % speed (alone: 50 %). No sprinting with it.
- More than 2.8 m apart and the helper loses their grip ("Auskemma!"). G lets go of your end; when the main carrier lets go, the helper carries on alone.
- A Bergmandl does not try to snatch what two miners carry.

### Health, death and spectating
- **Health belongs to the host.** In M2 the only way to die is a hard fall (landing faster than 8 m/s, about a 3.3 m drop; 12 health per m/s above that). The deadly creatures in M4 use the same path (`Game.host_damage_player`). Nobody gets hurt in the Gasthaus.
- **Death:** everything you carry drops, your body becomes a heavy loot item, "Hiniger Kumpl (Sterbegeld)", worth 60 S when it rides up (it can be carried together or wheeled in the Schubkarre). You sink to the floor, and after 2.5 s you watch a living teammate through their eyes; click or the mouse wheel switches between them.
- **Everybody dead:** 4 s later the lift goes up and the shift ends (solo: 4 s after your death).
- **Names:** players are "Kumpl 1–4" in the order they joined (Steam names come with M3). A name shows over a teammate's head only while you look right at them from up to 10 m, so it never lights up the dark.

### Player count
- **Scaling (§8) uses the players present when the lift goes down.** In the Gasthaus it follows joins and leaves, so the quota on the notice board changes when someone joins. A cycle's quota can therefore change between shifts if the crew changes.
- **The free Schubkarre** is a solo extra: a host who opens a multiplayer game does not get it, even before anyone joins.

### Testing
- `tools/mp_test.ps1 -Clients 3` now plays a short shift: the host waits for all clients (`--wait-peers=<n>`), rides down, rides up after a few seconds (`--auto-shift=<s>`), and every peer must have built the same mine.
- `tools/mp_test.ps1 -Coop` runs the scripted co-op scenario (`--scenario=coop` on the host, `coopclient` on the client): a falling item seen on the client, carrying together, hand-over, death and spectating.

## 2026-10-29 – M2 playtest feedback

### Chosen by Claude (change any time)
- **Your own lamp and the fog:** your own lamp lights the volumetric fog at 0.15 of its strength (others still see the full glow around your lamp). At full strength it hazed over the top third of your own screen.
- **Less bloom and a dimmer lamp:** mine glow intensity 0.4 (was 0.55), strength 0.8 (0.9), no bloom (was 0.04), only from HDR 1.6 up (was 1.2); lamp energy 1.9 (was 2.3).
- **Throw check:** `mp_test.ps1 -Coop` now throws a crystal while walking, once from the host and once from the client, and fails when the flight differs between the peers by more than 0.5 m. The reported "drops straight down on the other screen" is how the M1 build behaved (clients ran their own item physics without the throw speed); the M2 build passes this check headless and in real windows.

## 2026-10-29 – M3 Steam (chosen by Claude, change any time)

### GodotSteam
- **GodotSteam GDExtension 4.23.2** (Steamworks 1.65, from Codeberg, built for Godot 4.4+, works with 4.7.2) as a plain addon: the stock Godot editor and export templates keep working. Only the Windows x64 libraries are in the repo; the addon's editor updater plugin is left out (it would check the web every time the editor opens).
- **Transport:** `SteamMultiplayerPeer` with `server_relay`: clients connect only to the host (lobby owner), the host relays – the same star layout as ENet. Steam's relay network handles NAT.

### Lobbies
- **Hosting** creates a **friends-only** lobby for 4. "Für olle sichtbor" next to the host button (or `--public`) makes it public. Every lobby carries `game=soizwoed`, `ver=<version>` and `name=<host's Steam name>`.
- **Joining:** "Partien suachn" lists public Soizwöd lobbies of the same version (search filtered on `game` and `ver`) plus the lobbies Steam friends are in (also checked for `game` and `ver`), marked "(Freind)". Joining a lobby of another game or version is refused with a message.
- **Invites:** in the game, Esc → "Freind eiladn" opens the Steam overlay invite dialog (Shift+Tab → friends list works too). Accepting an invite or "Spiel beitreten" on a friend: if the game is running, it leaves the current session (also solo) and joins; if not, Steam starts it with `+connect_lobby <id>` and it joins right away.
- **Late join:** the host makes the lobby unjoinable while the crew is down the mine, and joinable again in the Gasthaus (the old refusal stays as a safety net).
- **Version:** `application/config/version` is now **0.3.0** (M3). Builds of different versions never see each other's lobbies.
- **Without Steam** (not running, or `--net=enet`) the menu shows the old address field and everything runs over ENet. Headless runs default to ENet, so automated tests never show the Steam account as "playing Spacewar"; `--net=steam` forces Steam.

### Names
- With Steam, players show their **Steam name** (nameplate, spectator line, "… is hin!"). Without Steam they stay "Kumpl 1–4".

### Proximity voice
- **Push-to-talk on V** by default; Esc → "Redn: Mikro offn" switches to open mic (Steam's voice detection only sends while you speak). The choice is saved in `user://settings.cfg`.
- Heard from the speaker's head: full volume close by, fading out to 30 m (`voice_chat_range`), **muffled through rock** (same check as creature voices), a little cave reverb on the Voice bus.
- **Dead players** are heard only by other dead players (without position, they are spectating); the dead still hear the living.
- **Shouting into the mic counts as "Plärren":** louder than `voice_shout_level` (0.6 peak) for 0.25 s scares Bergmandl, with the shout key's cooldown. (The Tatzelwurm in M4 will use the same loudness.)
- **"Mikro probiern"** in the Esc menu plays your own voice back to you, to check the mic alone.
- "Du redst…" bottom right while your voice goes out.
- **ENet sessions have no voice** (DESIGN.md §11 allows that).

### Testing
- `tools/steam_test.ps1` (Steam running, one account): a headless host creates a public lobby, a second instance must find exactly it with the filtered search, and joining it must be refused (own account). Real joining and voice need a second account on another PC.
